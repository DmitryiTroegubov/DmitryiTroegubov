## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🤖 OquBot
**Co-Founder · CTO · 3D Developer**

OquBot is an educational robotics platform built around modular animatronic kits that help students explore engineering, mechanics, electronics, and programming through hands-on assembly.

My work includes:

- Designing and iterating the majority of the project's **3D/CAD components**
- Developing parts of the **software architecture**
- Prototyping and preparing models for **3D printing**
- Building and maintaining the project's **website and infrastructure**
- Supporting product testing and development
- Leading technical/product presentations

### 🩸 Glucosense
**Independent Research & Engineering Project**

I independently worked on the complete development cycle:

`Hardware → Data Collection → Signal Processing → Modeling → Prototype → Validation`
